Privacy Policy for Numatica

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Numatica (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a calculator application developed by Jesper Laursen Thiesen. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. We have designed Numatica to collect as little data as possible.

Information we do not collect

Numatica has no user accounts and no analytics or tracking SDKs of our own. Your calculations, history, variables, and settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us. Diagnostic logs are written locally on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Numatica displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain information, including your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS), device type, IP address, and general ad-interaction data. This data is used by Google and its partners to deliver and measure advertising.

For how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

On Apple devices, Numatica asks for your permission before using the advertising identifier for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. Purchasing Numatica Pro removes all advertising, after which no ad-related data is collected.

Consent (EEA, UK, and applicable US states)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Numatica presents a consent form (via Google’s User Messaging Platform) before serving personalized ads, in accordance with GDPR. You may change or withdraw your consent at any time from within the app. Residents of certain US states are shown an equivalent privacy notice.

In-app purchases

Numatica Pro is sold as an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple’s handling of that transaction is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Numatica is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most such requests relating to advertising should be directed to Google using the links above. You may also contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: numatica@fastmail.com.
